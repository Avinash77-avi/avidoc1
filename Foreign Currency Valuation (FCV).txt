Foreign Currency Valuation (FCV)
---

## 🌍 Context: Foreign Currency Valuation (FCV) in SAP FICO
- FCV is performed at **month-end or year-end** to revalue open items (like receivables, payables, or bank balances) in foreign currency.
- The system compares the **transaction exchange rate** with the **closing exchange rate**.
- Differences are posted as **realized gains/losses** (when payment is made) or **unrealized gains/losses** (when revaluation is done but not yet settled).

---

## 📊 Example Scenario
- Vendor invoice booked: **USD 1,000**
- Exchange rate at posting: **1 USD = 80 INR**
- Invoice amount in INR: **80,000 INR**
- Payment date exchange rate: **1 USD = 82 INR**
- Payment amount in INR: **82,000 INR**

---

## 🧾 SAP FICO Entries

### 1. At Invoice Posting
```
Dr Expense A/c                  80,000 INR
    Cr Vendor A/c (USD)         80,000 INR
```

### 2. At Payment (Realized Gain/Loss)
Payment made at 82,000 INR, but liability was 80,000 INR → **2,000 INR loss**.

```
Dr Vendor A/c                   80,000 INR
Dr Exchange Loss A/c             2,000 INR
    Cr Bank A/c                  82,000 INR
```

If instead the rate was **78 INR**, then you’d have a **realized gain**:

```
Dr Vendor A/c                   80,000 INR
    Cr Bank A/c                  78,000 INR
    Cr Exchange Gain A/c          2,000 INR
```

---

## 🔑 Key Points in SAP FICO FCV
- **Unrealized gains/losses** are posted during revaluation runs (month-end).
- **Realized gains/losses** are posted automatically when the foreign currency item is cleared (payment or receipt).
- Accounts used:
  - **Exchange Gain A/c**
  - **Exchange Loss A/c**
  - Defined in **OB09 (Account Determination for Exchange Differences)**.





 **unrealized gain/loss entries for vendor open items** during **Foreign Currency Valuation (FCV)** in SAP FICO.

---

## 📊 Example Scenario
- Vendor invoice: **USD 1,000**  
- Exchange rate at invoice posting: **1 USD = 80 INR** → **80,000 INR**  
- Closing rate at month-end: **1 USD = 82 INR** → **82,000 INR**  
- Difference: **+2,000 INR (Unrealized Loss)**  

---

## 🧾 SAP FICO Accounting Entries

### 1. At Invoice Posting
```
Dr Expense A/c                  80,000 INR
    Cr Vendor A/c               80,000 INR
```

### 2. At Month-End FCV (Unrealized Loss)
System revalues the open vendor item at closing rate (82 INR).  
Liability increases by 2,000 INR → Unrealized loss.

```
Dr Unrealized Exchange Loss A/c   2,000 INR
    Cr Vendor A/c (Payables)      2,000 INR
```

*(This entry is reversed automatically at the start of the next period.)*

---

## 📈 Unrealized Gain Example
If the closing rate was **1 USD = 78 INR** → **78,000 INR**, liability decreases by 2,000 INR → Unrealized gain.

```
Dr Vendor A/c (Payables)          2,000 INR
    Cr Unrealized Exchange Gain A/c   2,000 INR
```










FCV FOR CUSTOMER:- 


**customer payment scenario** in SAP FICO where a **realized foreign exchange gain** occurs during clearing.  

---

## 📊 Example Scenario
- Customer invoice: **USD 1,000**
- Exchange rate at invoice posting: **1 USD = 80 INR**
- Invoice amount in INR: **80,000 INR**
- Exchange rate at payment receipt: **1 USD = 82 INR**
- Payment received in INR: **82,000 INR**

Here, the company gains **2,000 INR** because the customer paid at a stronger rate than when the invoice was booked.

---

## 🧾 SAP FICO Accounting Entries

### 1. At Invoice Posting
```
Dr Customer A/c (Debtors)       80,000 INR
    Cr Revenue A/c              80,000 INR
```

### 2. At Customer Payment (Realized Gain)
Payment received at 82,000 INR, but receivable was 80,000 INR → **2,000 INR gain**.

```
Dr Bank A/c                     82,000 INR
    Cr Customer A/c             80,000 INR
    Cr Exchange Gain A/c         2,000 INR
```
